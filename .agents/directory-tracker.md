## Backlink verification

Once a listing goes live, verify the backlink is dofollow:
1. Open the live listing page
2. Right-click your product link, Inspect, check for `rel="nofollow"` — if absent, it is dofollow
3. Or: `curl -sIL [listing-url] | grep -i link`
4. Re-verify all live listings quarterly — directories sometimes silently switch to nofollow
