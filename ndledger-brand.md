# NDLedger — brand and messaging philosophy

Read this file before executing any marketing, content, copy, or community task for NDLedger.

---

## Product truth

NDLedger is NOT:
- A note-taking app
- A chat archive
- A generic productivity tool

NDLedger IS:
> The external memory that makes sure your best AI thinking survives your own brain.

The workflow is:
1. Capture (paste or record a conversation)
2. Extract (AI identifies decisions, tasks, insights)
3. Organise (library + mind map)
4. Retrieve (search + reuse)

All messaging must reinforce this loop. Never describe the mechanism without naming the promise.

---

## Core purpose statement (locked)

> NDLedger is the external memory that makes sure your best AI thinking survives your own brain.

This is the single sentence everything else hangs off. Not a filing system. Not a productivity tool. A second memory — one that doesn't forget, doesn't lose the thread, and doesn't require the user to be organised to use it.

## Target audience (strict)

Focus ONLY on:
- The ADHD/autism, neurodivergent population
- People who use AI frequently (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.)
- People who lose track of valuable outputs
- Founders, operators, and knowledge workers
- People who want structure, clarity, and recall

Do NOT:
- Target everyone
- Lead with clinical labels
- Use vague "productivity" language

---

## Neurodivergent messaging rules (mandatory — carry into all content)

These rules apply to every piece of content, every directory listing, every social post, every onboarding screen.

1. Never frame the product as fixing disorganisation.
   The user isn't broken. A neurotypical system is failing them. NDLedger is the external system doing its job.

2. Name the retrieval moment specifically.
   "When you need it" is generic. The real moment is: starting a new AI session and knowing you already worked this out — but not being able to find it.

3. Lead with zero effort required.
   No tagging, no filing, no decisions before value. Any friction before the first extraction is a drop-off risk for this audience.

4. Use "second brain" framing sparingly but precisely.
   The ND audience knows this concept deeply. Use it in community channels — not on the landing page where it risks feeling clinical.

5. Respect the depth, don't condescend to the chaos.
   This user generates exceptional insight. Tone should honour the quality of their thinking — not position them as someone who needs help staying organised.

---

## Session rules (non-negotiable)

- AU English throughout
- No em dashes
- No "free to use" or "free forever" — use "free to get started"
- No upload references — users paste or record conversations
- Deletion applies to transcripts, not topics
- Seven categories (six business + one personal)
- CTA button copy is "Get Started"
- Paid tiers are planned — do not revert to free-forever framing

---

## CTA

Single primary CTA across all channels: Get Started

---

## Success metrics (track only these)

- Activation rate (% of users who paste/record + retrieve)
- Time to first value
- Retention (repeat usage)
- Conversion (free to paid)

Ignore vanity metrics unless they directly drive activation.

---

## Decision filter

Before executing any task, ask:
- Does this help users stop losing valuable AI outputs?
- Does this make the product clearer or more complicated?
- Does this move users toward first successful retrieval?

If not — do not proceed.
